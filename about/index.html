We build software that helps businesses grow.

Zamra Tech is an IT services company with offices in the USA, UK, UAE and Pakistan. We design and develop websites, mobile apps, games and custom software — and provide skilled engineers to extend in-house teams.

Technology partners, not just vendors

We help businesses thrive online with web design, mobile app development, software development and more. Our team is committed to delivering solutions that drive results and improve the experience for your users.

Our web work produces sites that look sharp and are built for engagement. Our mobile apps run seamlessly on iOS and Android. Our custom software streamlines operations around your exact processes, and our game studio crafts immersive experiences for players worldwide.

After launch we stay involved: managed cloud hosting keeps your apps secure and scalable, and maintenance plans keep everything up to date. White label products help partners get to market faster, and our IoT work connects devices to data-driven insights.

Most of all, we aim for lasting partnerships — giving clients the technology and the people they need to keep moving forward.
