Marketing FAQ

Have another question? Contact us or book a call.

How soon will I see results?

PPC and paid social can bring traffic and leads within days of launch. SEO and ASO build momentum over time — most clients see clear movement in 3–6 months, growing from there.

Do you need a long contract?

No. Marketing retainers run month to month after an initial setup period. We earn the next month by showing results in this one.

Is the ad budget included in your fee?

No. Your ad spend is paid directly to Google, Meta and other platforms from your own accounts, so you always own the accounts and the data. Our fee covers strategy, setup, management and reporting.

Can you market an app or website you didn't build?

Yes. We start with a free audit of your site or app, tracking and current campaigns, then recommend the quickest wins.

What will I see in your reports?

A monthly report with the numbers that matter for your goals — traffic, rankings, installs, leads, cost per acquisition and return on ad spend — plus what we did and what we'll do next.

Can you work with our in-house marketing team?

Absolutely. We can run specific channels, support your team with design and development, or train them to take campaigns over.
