Questions we often hear

How long does a typical web project take?

A marketing site usually takes 3–6 weeks. A custom web application typically runs 2–6 months depending on scope. We give you a dated milestone plan after discovery.

Will I own the source code?

Yes. Code, designs and accounts are transferred to you, and we work in a repository you control from day one.

Can you take over an existing site or app?

Yes. We start with a code and security audit, fix the urgent issues, then agree a roadmap for improvements.

Do you handle hosting and maintenance after launch?

We can. Our managed cloud hosting and maintenance plans cover uptime monitoring, updates, backups and small enhancements.
