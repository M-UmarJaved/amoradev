Engagement models that fit how you work.

Start with one engineer or a full team. Switch models as your needs change — there's no long-term lock-in.

Full-time

160 hrs / month

A dedicated engineer working only on your product.

  • Joins your daily stand-ups
  • Guaranteed time-zone overlap
  • Best for long-term roadmaps

Dedicated team

3+ people

A cross-functional pod with developers, QA and a lead.

  • Team lead or delivery manager
  • Shared velocity and reporting
  • Best for new products or modules

Part-time

80 hrs / month

Specialist help without a full-time commitment.

  • Ideal for DevOps, QA, design
  • Fixed weekly schedule
  • Upgrade to full-time any time

  • Pre-paid hour blocks
  • Timesheets with every invoice
  • Best for ad-hoc work
