Add vetted developers, designers and QA engineers to your in-house team. They work on your roadmap, in your tools and time zone — we take care of recruiting, payroll, equipment and HR.

Why companies augment with Zamra Tech

Hiring good engineers takes months. Augmentation gets the right people working on your product now, with less risk.

Start in days, not months

Skip job ads and long hiring cycles. We already have the talent; you choose who fits.

Vetted for skill and fit

Every engineer passes technical assessments, communication checks and a culture interview before you see them.

Scale up or down freely

Add people for a launch, release them when the push is over. No long-term lock-in.

You stay in control

Secure and compliant

How it works

A simple five-step process from first call to a productive team member.

  1. Share your needs

    Tell us the roles, skills, seniority and time-zone overlap you need.

  2. Get matched profiles

    We shortlist pre-vetted engineers who fit your stack and culture.

  3. Interview & choose

    Run your own interviews and tests. You only pick people you're sure about.

  4. Onboard in days

    We sign NDAs, set up equipment and accounts; they join your stand-ups.

  5. Scale as you go

    Add or release people with notice. A dedicated manager keeps things smooth.

    Staff augmentation FAQ

    Still have questions? Contact us or book a call.

    What is staff augmentation?

    You add our engineers to your existing team for as long as you need them. They work in your tools, attend your stand-ups and report to your managers — we handle hiring, payroll, equipment and HR.

    How quickly can a developer start?

    We usually share matched profiles within a few days of understanding your requirements. Once you approve a candidate, onboarding can start straight away.

    Can I interview the developers first?

    Always. You review CVs, run your own technical interviews and only work with people you approve.

    What if a developer isn't the right fit?

    Tell us and we'll arrange a replacement at no extra cost, with a knowledge handover to keep your work moving.

    Which time zones do you cover?

    Our team works with clients across the US, UK, Europe and the Middle East, with guaranteed overlap hours agreed in your contract.

    Who owns the code and IP?

    You do. All work product and IP belong to you, and every engineer signs NDA and IP assignment agreements.

    Can I scale the team up or down?

    Yes. Add people as your roadmap grows or reduce headcount with notice — no long-term lock-in.

    How is billing handled?

    One simple monthly invoice per engineer or team, based on the engagement model you choose. No recruitment fees.

    Ready to add engineers to your team?

    Tell us who you need. We'll share vetted profiles and transparent monthly rates.
